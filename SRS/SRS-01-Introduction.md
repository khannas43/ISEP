## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for the IMO Strategic Engagement Platform (ISEP) — a centralised digital platform for India's engagement with the International Maritime Organization (IMO), International Labour Organization (ILO), and International Mobile Satellite Organization (IMSO).

ISEP is being developed for the Directorate General of Shipping (DGS), Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways (MoPSW), Government of India.

---

## 2. Scope

ISEP digitises the end-to-end workflow of India's international maritime engagement — from agenda distribution and document management through collaborative drafting, multi-level approval, live meeting collaboration, and post-meeting analytics and archival.

The platform serves internal DGS stakeholders, Mercantile Marine Department (MMD) officials, MoPSW representatives, and inter-ministerial agencies (e.g., MoEFCC, MEA) in the role of external consultees.

---

## 3. Objectives

1. Digitise the complete workflow: agenda distribution → drafting → review → approval → submission
2. Standardise processes for Correspondence Groups, Working Groups, and inter-ministerial consultations
3. Maintain a complete audit trail of revisions, discussions, comments, and approvals
4. Centralise archival of IMO documents, country positions, meeting minutes, and intervention statements
5. Enable long-term knowledge continuity within DGS and support institutional memory
6. Improve efficiency, reduce duplication, and enhance accuracy of submissions through digital automation
7. Strengthen capabilities through training, workshops, and user familiarisation
8. Align with Government of India digital governance standards, MeitY cloud guidelines, and data security norms

## 4. Document Conventions

- **Must / Shall** — mandatory requirement

- All monetary values in INR unless otherwise stated
- Confidentiality: firm name and client name are not used in documentation — always "the firm" and "the client" or "DGS"
